Fourteen objectives guide every program we run — from the badminton court to the seniors' centre to the next community fundraiser.

Our objectives

Guided by service,
diversity & active living.

At STARS Club, St. Albert, we are committed to building a strong, inclusive, and thriving community. These are the fourteen objectives that guide everything we do.

01 Service

Strengthen communities through service

Promote community building, social services, and charitable initiatives across Canada under the auspices of the club.

02 Inclusion

Celebrate diversity and inclusion

Create meaningful opportunities for social, cultural, and multicultural engagement that bring people together.

03 Wellbeing

Support mental health and well-being

Enhance social well-being and mental health to cultivate a healthy, positive, and vibrant community.

04 Care

Care for seniors & differently abled individuals

Support the needs and dignity of senior citizens and differently abled individuals within St. Albert and surrounding areas.

05 Relief

Provide emergency & humanitarian support

Offer timely assistance to individuals and communities affected by natural disasters through volunteer efforts and donations to qualified beneficiaries, including registered charities.

06 Volunteering

Promote a culture of volunteering

Encourage, train, and coordinate volunteer activities in the socio-cultural sector as a charitable organization.

07 Connection

Connect locally and globally

Use digital platforms and social media to share cultural stories and community achievements worldwide, fostering collaboration with international partners.

08 Facilities

Develop community spaces

Establish, maintain, and operate community centres and recreational facilities for the benefit and enjoyment of the wider community.

09 Facilities

Build and maintain facilities

Acquire land and develop buildings and facilities for recreational, sports, cultural, and community purposes through purchase, lease, gift, or other means.

10 Sport

Promote sports and active living

Encourage physical activity and sports development among youth, supporting healthy lifestyles and the growth of young athletes.

11 Culture

Host and participate in events

Plan, organize, host, and participate in sports and cultural events at provincial, national, and international levels.

12 Education

Support education and excellence

Offer awards, scholarships, prizes, medals, and educational assistance to deserving members of the community.

13 Digital

Advance digital literacy

Embrace emerging technologies to enhance advanced digital skills and digital literacy among community members.

14 Growth

Ensure sustainable growth

Seek and secure funding through government grants, private sponsorships, community donations, and other mission-aligned revenue-generating activities.
