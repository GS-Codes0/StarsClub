Who we are

Welcome to STARS Club

STARS Club St. Albert is a community-focused organization committed to building a healthy, inclusive, and vibrant society through sports, healthy living, and meaningful community service.

We promote physical activity and sports development for youth and adults, supporting active lifestyles that benefit physical, mental, and social well-being — and beyond fitness, we give back by supporting seniors, differently abled individuals, and those in need through charitable, volunteer-led programs.

As a newly registered organization, STARS Club welcomes funding, donations, and partnerships to help establish recreational facilities and deliver programs that improve quality of life across St. Albert and surrounding areas.
